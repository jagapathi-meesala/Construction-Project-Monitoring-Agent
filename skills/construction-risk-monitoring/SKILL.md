# Construction Risk Monitoring
## Purpose
Evaluate documented construction risks and safety observations using transparent scoring bands.
## Inputs
Risk description, probability, impact, or safety observation severity.
## Processing
Risk score is probability multiplied by impact; safety priority follows the supplied severity band.
## Outputs
Risk band, response guidance, or safety follow-up priority.
## Limitations
Scoring is a monitoring aid and is not a substitute for a qualified site safety assessment.
## Expected behavior
Reject malformed values and preserve traceable source observations.
